Metaheuristic-Based Feature Selection for Induction Motor Fault Diagnosis

Compared four metaheuristic optimisers (WOA, HHO, EO, MVO) paired with three gradient-boosting classifiers on 2.1M sensor samples across 5 fault classes of a 110 kW motor. Cut features from 26 to as few as 3 while keeping near-perfect accuracy; best model was EO + XGBoost. The search ran on a stratified 5% subset to save compute. Accepted at an international conference.

Motor Predictive Maintenance

Live condition monitoring for a small AC motor. An ESP32-S3 node streams vibration, current, speed, temperature and sound every 2 s over MQTT. A Python service extracts features, scores each reading with an anomaly model, and pushes it to a live web dashboard that works on PC or phone. A built-in dummy motor lets you inject faults for demos.
